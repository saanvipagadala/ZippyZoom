Welcome to Zippy Zoom Mini Elevator

Thanks for exploring the world of robotics with us!

Zippy Zoom Mini Elevator is a fun and educational app that lets kids and curious minds control a mini elevator from their phone.

Learn how stepper motors work, experiment with engineering concepts, and discover the excitement of hands-on STEM!

If you have any questions, feedback, or need help with the app, we're here for you.

Three motors. One elevator. A lot of learning.

What started as a simple elevator project turned into a deep learning experience about motors, control systems, and user-friendly design. Watch how I tested different motors, fixed real problems, and ended up with a Bluetooth-controlled mini elevator.

Let's discover and create amazing machines, one floor at a time!

Features

Discover the exciting world of robotics — your mini elevator in one fun, interactive app!

With precise stepper motor movement and simple floor-by-floor control, Zippy Zoom: Mini Elevator lets kids and curious minds experiment with engineering and STEM concepts. Whether you're a budding engineer, a curious student, or a lifelong learner, this app makes learning about robotics hands-on, fun, and inspiring.

  • Phone Control: Move the elevator between floors with a simple tap
  • Stepper Motor Precision: Learn how stepper motors make accurate movement possible
  • Hands-On Learning: Experiment with engineering concepts and problem-solving
  • Kid-Friendly Design: Simple, colorful, and fun interface for all ages
  • STEM Exploration: Discover how design and control choices affect real-world machines
